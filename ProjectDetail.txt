1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURAL FOUNDATION
====================================================================================================

1.1 Domain Summary:
The Hotel Management System (HMS) is an enterprise-grade, distributed microservices platform designed
to manage the entire operational lifecycle of hotel and hospitality chains. It coordinates guest
reservations, room inventory, dining & guest experience, housekeeping & maintenance operations,
staff administration & time tracking, procurement & vendor logistics, billing & payments, reporting,
and automated real-time event-driven notifications.

1.2 Technology Stack:
- Runtime & Core: Java 21 (LTS), Spring Boot 3.2+
- Microservices Framework: Spring Cloud (2023.x Release Train)
- Service Discovery: Spring Cloud Netflix Eureka (`eureka-server` on port 8761)
- API Gateway: Spring Cloud Gateway (`api-gateway` on port 8080) with route-level security
- Centralized Configuration: Spring Cloud Config Server (`config-server` on port 8888) with Git/Local config-repo
- Inter-Service RPC: Spring Cloud OpenFeign with Resilience4j Circuit Breakers & Fallbacks
- Asynchronous Messaging: RabbitMQ Message Broker (`hms.events.exchange` Topic Exchange)
- Security: Spring Security 6.x + Stateless JWT Authentication (HMAC-SHA512 with 512-bit key)
- Database & Persistence: MySQL 8.x per-service database pattern with Spring Data JPA & Hibernate
- Documentation: SpringDoc OpenAPI 3.0 / Swagger UI on every microservice

2. USER ROLES & OPERATIONAL PERMISSIONS
====================================================================================================

The system implements strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Every JWT token carries the user's
unique identifier (`sub` / `userId`), username, email, and assigned `role`.

2.1 User Role Catalog:
1. OWNER:
   - Supreme system authority.
   - Access to financial balance sheets, profit & loss, high-level staff salary details, vendor contracts,
     hotel policy changes, and executive dashboards.
2. ADMIN:
   - Full technical and administrative control over hotel configuration.
   - Manages user accounts, staff profiles, system settings, room configurations, pricing policies,
     and overall audit logs.
3. MANAGER:
   - Department and operational lead (Front Desk Manager, Housekeeping Manager, F&B Manager, General Manager).
   - Approves Purchase Requests (PR), Purchase Orders (PO), Staff Leave applications.
   - Manages shift allocations, room maintenance approvals, bill adjustments, and guest disputes.
4. RECEPTIONIST:
   - Front-desk operations team.
   - Creates guest profiles, performs check-ins and check-outs, issues room keys, adds folio charges,
     finalizes bills, and coordinates with housekeeping for clean rooms.
5. HOUSEKEEPER:
   - Room cleaning, sanitization, and room turnover staff.
   - Updates room statuses (`CLEAN`, `DIRTY`, `INSPECTED`, `DO_NOT_DISTURB`), raises maintenance requests,
     submits material requests (e.g. linen/towels), clocks in/out.
6. MAINTENANCE:
   - Facility and engineering technicians.
   - Resolves room/amenity work orders (`IN_REPAIR`, `OUT_OF_ORDER`), consumes maintenance parts,
     raises low-stock purchase requests, clocks in/out.
7. CHEF / KITCHEN:
   - Restaurant and kitchen management.
   - Receives Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT), updates dish statuses, manages kitchen pantry stock.
8. WAITER:
   - Dining hall and in-room service staff.
   - Takes table orders, delivers dining requests, posts restaurant charges to guest room folio.
9. CASHIER:
   - Billing and treasury staff.
   - Processes guest final payments, logs cash/card transactions, issues official receipts, handles refunds.
10. STAFF:
    - Generic employee identity.
    - View own profile, shift schedule, clock-in, clock-out, apply for personal leave.
11. GUEST:
    - Registered hotel guest.
    - Searches available rooms, books reservations, views own folio, orders room service/spa, submits feedback.

4. COMPREHENSIVE END-TO-END BUSINESS WORKFLOWS
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 1: COMPLETE GUEST RESERVATION, STAY, CHECKOUT & BILLING FINALIZATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Guest Discovery & Reservation
- Guest or Receptionist searches available rooms via `GET /api/rooms/available?checkIn=2026-10-01&checkOut=2026-10-05`.
- Reservation is created via `POST /api/reservations` in `CONFIRMED` status.
- RabbitMQ publishes `ReservationCreated` event.
- `billing-service` receives the event and initializes a Folio / Bill record in `DRAFT` status with room rate totals.

Step 2: Guest Check-In
- Upon arrival, Receptionist verifies guest identity and calls `PUT /api/reservations/{id}/check-in`.
- Room status in `room-service` transitions from `AVAILABLE` to `OCCUPIED`.
- Keycard access is generated.

Step 3: In-Stay Service & Incidentals Posting
- Guest orders room service food ($45.00) via `POST /api/dining/orders`.
- The charge is posted to `billing-service` via `POST /api/bills/charges`, updating the folio with Item: "Dinner Room Service", Subtotal: $45.00, Tax: $4.50.

Step 4: Check-Out Request & Bill Finalization
- Guest approaches Front Desk to check out.
- Receptionist reviews the bill: Total = Room ($400.00) + Dining ($49.50) = $449.50.
- Receptionist locks all charges and finalizes the bill via `PUT /api/bills/{billId}/finalize`.
- Status changes to `FINALIZED`. At this point, the outstanding balance is $449.50.

Step 5: Payment Settlement
- Cashier/Receptionist calls `POST /api/payments` with `billId: 101, amount: 449.50, paymentMethod: "CREDIT_CARD"`.
- Payment Service verifies the transaction and synchronously updates `billing-service` via Feign.
- In `billing-service`, `paidAmount` becomes $449.50, `outstandingAmount` becomes $0.00, and status transitions to `PAID`.
- Official payment receipt is generated.

Step 6: Physical Check-Out & Housekeeping Dispatch
- Receptionist marks reservation as `CHECKED_OUT` via `PUT /api/reservations/{id}/check-out`.
- Room status in `room-service` transitions to `DIRTY`.
- RabbitMQ event `GuestCheckedOut` triggers an automated Housekeeping Cleaning Task in `operations-service`.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 2: STAFF HR, ROSTERING, ATTENDANCE & LEAVE MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Staff Onboarding
- Admin/Owner creates employee profile via `POST /api/staff`.
- Assigns role (`RECEPTIONIST`, `HOUSEKEEPER`, `MAINTENANCE`, `MANAGER`), salary, shift, and department.

Step 2: Daily Clock-In
- Employee arrives at shift and clocks in via `POST /api/attendance/check-in/{staffId}`.
- System validates employee is `active == true`, matches user credentials, records `checkInTime = LocalTime.now()`, and status = `PRESENT`.

Step 3: Cross-Midnight / Night Shift Clock-Out
- Employee finishes shift and clocks out via `POST /api/attendance/check-out/{staffId}`.
- If employee clocked in at 10:00 PM yesterday and clocks out at 6:00 AM today, the system detects the open check-in from yesterday, sets `checkOutTime = 06:00:00`, and calculates `workHours = 8.00 hours`.

Step 4: Leave Application & Automatic Roster Synchronization
- Receptionist/Housekeeper submits leave via `POST /api/leave` (e.g., from 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-12).
- Admin or Owner approves leave via `PUT /api/leave/{id}/approval` (`status: "APPROVED"`).
- `LeaveServiceImpl` iterates over all dates in the range (Oct 10, 11, 12) and automatically populates `attendance` records with `status = ON_LEAVE` and remarks "Approved Leave: ANNUAL".

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW 3: PROCUREMENT, LOW-STOCK ALERTS, PURCHASE ORDERS & INVENTORY REPLENISHMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Automated Low-Stock Alert
- Housekeeping staff consumes 20 bath towels via `POST /api/inventory/stock-out`.
- Warehouse stock drops to 5, which is below the minimum threshold of 10.
- `inventory-service` publishes `LowStockDetected` event to RabbitMQ (`hms.inventory.low-stock`).

Step 2: Auto-Generation of Purchase Request (PR)
- `purchase-service` consumes `LowStockDetected` event.
- Automatically creates a draft Purchase Request (PR) with `priority = HIGH`, requesting 15 replenishment units.

Step 3: PR Approval & Supplier Quotations
- Hotel Manager reviews PR via `GET /api/purchase-requests` and approves it via `PUT /api/purchase-requests/{id}/approve`.
- Supplier bids are received and logged via `POST /api/quotations`.
- Manager compares price, tax, and lead times, then selects the best quote via `PUT /api/quotations/{id}/select`.

Step 4: PO Creation & Dispatch
- Manager generates Purchase Order via `POST /api/purchase-orders`, approves it (`PUT .../approve`), and dispatches it (`PUT .../send`).
- System prevents orders from being issued to `BLACKLISTED` or `INACTIVE` suppliers.

Step 5: Goods Receipt (GRN) & Automated Real-Time Inventory Stock-In
- Vendor truck delivers goods to the loading dock.
- Staff records Goods Receipt Note via `POST /api/goods-receipts`: Received = 15, Accepted = 14, Rejected = 1 (damaged).
- `purchase-service` publishes `GoodsReceived` event to RabbitMQ.
- `inventory-service` consumes the event and automatically executes `inventoryService.stockIn(...)`, incrementing physical warehouse balance by +14 units without manual inventory intervention.
